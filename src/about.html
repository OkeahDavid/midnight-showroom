Midnight Showroom

A night-time car showroom in the browser. Each car flies together part by part as a wireframe, gets painted in by a scanning light, drives in and out as you switch, and comes with sourced specs and real colours.

How it works

  • Rendering. three.js draws the cars in a custom studio lit by soft light strips. Only the lamps and the floor ring glow.
  • Motion. anime.js drives the assembly, the scan, the drive-in and the scroll-linked camera.
  • Models. Each model is compressed to a few megabytes with glTF Transform. Every part is kept separate so the car can be taken apart and its wheels can roll.
  • Data. Specs, history and paint lists live in one data file, so adding a car means adding one entry and its model.
